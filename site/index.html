Ubuntu dotfiles installer

Pick what you want from ronaldo-kwan/dotfiles (Ubuntu 26.04, GNOME, Catppuccin) and get an installer for a fresh Ubuntu: a one-line command, or a file for Ubuntu's automated install. Everything happens in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

1 What to set up

Switch off what you don't want. Unticked parts are skipped, and dot check won't expect them later.
